sample data · kg CO₂e / month

Sustainability Tracker

Full-stack carbon tracker. Turns logged activity, CSV uploads and PDF utility invoices into CO₂e data with background workers, then shows it on a dashboard.

Companies log activity across Transport, Energy, Food and Waste, and a custom engine converts it to CO₂e. Celery workers ingest CSV uploads (pandas) and pull structured data out of PDF utility invoices (pdfplumber + regex). The dashboard has Recharts visuals, eco-score, streak badges, and a rule-based advice engine for month-over-month reduction tips. The whole stack runs in Docker Compose.
